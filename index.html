  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or file manager
  3. If prompted, allow installation from unknown sources: Go to Settings > Security (or Apps & notifications) and enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser
  4. Tap "Install" and wait for the installation to complete

Critical Rules — Read Before Proceeding

FAKE / STAGED TASKS — BIGGEST PROBLEM

Some people fake tasks to get paid. Examples: wiping a plate that is already clean for two minutes straight, transferring water between bottles back and forth for no reason, carrying the same item from one spot to another repeatedly, opening and closing drawers with no purpose. If the task looks pointless or made up, REJECT it.

SCREEN RECORDING SCAM

Some people point the camera at a TV, computer, or phone screen instead of doing a real task. If you see another monitor or screen visible anywhere in the video, the person may be recording someone else's footage off a display. This is a common scam. REJECT immediately.

NOT A ROBOT-WORTHY TASK

The video must show a real task with economic or household value: cooking, cleaning dishes, repairing, organizing, sorting. REJECT: reading, writing notes, drinking coffee, scrolling phone, watching TV.

WHEN IN DOUBT, TAP NO

It is always better to reject a questionable video than to approve a bad one.
